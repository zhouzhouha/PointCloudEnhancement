Final Point Cloud Enhancement Papers To Check

Updated 2026-07-23 after confirming SuperPC as read, included in the survey, and added to Overleaf. This is the canonical merged reading list. The primary benchmark remains point-cloud-only; multimodal methods are labeled and reported separately.

Current decision: the table below is the working included survey list. It combines denoising, color/texture enhancement, upsampling, completion, joint upsampling/cleaning, compression-derived enhancement, and explicitly labeled multimodal restoration methods. Grad-PU is confirmed as method #20 in the original Final-20 sequence. Later user decisions add Deep-RS, SAPCU, PD-LTS, PathNet, APU-LDI, AdaPoinTr, and SuperPC without displacing that original numbering. “Self-Supervised Arbitrary-Scale Implicit Point Clouds Upsampling” is the extension of the SAPCU conference work and is counted as the same method/paper family, not as an additional method. SuperPC requires a separate multimodal comparison; FBNet remains excluded by scope, and TULIP remains removed.
