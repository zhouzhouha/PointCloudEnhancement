Final Point Cloud Enhancement Papers To Check

Updated 2026-06-22 for the UVG-CWI-DQPC OrangeKettlebell selected-10 benchmark. This is the canonical merged reading list; the older dated duplicate has been removed. Scope: point-cloud-only enhancement, degraded point cloud in and enhanced point cloud out.

Current decision: the six methods that were in Strong Candidates To Read For Final 20, plus PC2-PU, are now included in the survey paper. SuperPC and FBNet remain excluded by scope; TULIP has been removed because it is LiDAR range-image upsampling, not direct point-cloud enhancement for dense RGB point clouds.
